The Hot Seat Board

Firing probability for all 67 Power-Four head coaches, ranked within each conference. One composite estimate per coach, built from four inputs — buyout reality, the CBS Sports hot-seat consensus (0–5), athletic-director alignment, and the 2026 schedule. The heat bar under each name maps to the probability, so the genuine danger glows and the safe tail cools out. Coaches at 5%+ risk carry an underlined, linked name — open any for the full scouting note.

Two per league — except one

Every conference has exactly two coaches above 30% … except the ACC, which stacks four (Norvell, Belichick, Doeren, O'Brien). The other three leagues each funnel their real risk onto a single pair, while more than a third of every roster is first-year hires who can't be fired on performance.

The five hottest seats

Fickell (58), Aranda (55), Norvell (48), Locksley (50), Beamer (35) are the national top five, and each is anchored to a CBS rating of 4.3 or higher. In every case the deciding variable isn't the record — it's whether the school can afford the buyout.

Loud names, cold seats

A high rating isn't a pink slip. Deion Sanders (3.1 → 4%), Dabo Swinney (3.1 → 4%) and Kalen DeBoer (3.0 → 3%) all draw heat in the polls but sit behind buyout fortresses of $33M, ~$57M and ~$67M. The money, not the noise, sets the number.

Probabilities are holistic estimates, not outputs of a formal statistical model — a single season-level figure that won't time a midseason firing (a 1–5 start could end things in October). Voluntary departures for health or a better job are excluded; this measures performance-based dismissal only. First-year coaches (FY) carry effectively zero 2026 firing risk regardless of record.
